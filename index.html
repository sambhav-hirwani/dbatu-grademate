    About DBATU GradeMate

    DBATU GradeMate is a free SGPA and CGPA calculator designed for students of Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University. It follows the official credit-based grading system and helps students calculate GPA quickly and accurately.

    How to Calculate SGPA in DBATU

    SGPA is calculated by multiplying grade points with subject credits, summing them, and dividing by total credits. This tool automates the process to save time and avoid errors.
